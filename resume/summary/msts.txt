As a Senior Data Scientist at the Nevada National Security Site, I specialize
in developing and deploying advanced machine learning models using Python and
PyTorch to drive data-informed decisions across various operational processes.
My contributions have included developing object-detection models with YOLO,
crafting LSTM variational-autoencoders for precise anomaly detection, and
creating a U-Net based image deblurring model. My role extends beyond
technical development to enhancing collaboration with non-technical staff,
given them the power to leverage these technologies for improved decision-making and
operational efficiency. This engagement has also involved mentoring interns and
creating a user-friendly Django web interface for more accessible management of
a Neo4j graph database.
